<div class="page-intro">
<p class="section-index">RESEARCH</p>
<h1>From human understanding to physical collaboration.</h1>
<p>Our research connects perception, embodied learning, and robot intelligence to create systems that can operate naturally and effectively alongside people.</p>
</div>

<div class="focus-list">
<article class="focus-row">
<div class="focus-index">01</div>
<div><p class="focus-kicker">Perceive / Reason / Understand</p><h2>Understand Humans</h2><p>Robots need more than sensors: they need representations that connect people, places, language, and intent. We study robust perception and spatial intelligence for understanding dynamic human environments.</p><p class="topic-line">Perception · Spatial Intelligence · State Estimation · Language · SLAM · Navigation</p></div>
</article>
<article class="focus-row">
<div class="focus-index">02</div>
<div><p class="focus-kicker">Observe / Imitate / Generalize</p><h2>Learn from Humans</h2><p>Human demonstrations contain rich knowledge about motion, contact, and task structure. We develop embodied learning methods that transform these demonstrations into transferable robot skills.</p><p class="topic-line">Imitation Learning · Human Motion · Embodied Learning · Humanoid Robotics</p></div>
</article>
<article class="focus-row">
<div class="focus-index">03</div>
<div><p class="focus-kicker">Plan / Act / Collaborate</p><h2>Collaborate with Humans</h2><p>We build robots that coordinate their decisions and physical actions with people. Our goal is safe, legible, and capable collaboration across shared spaces and shared tasks.</p><p class="topic-line">Human-Robot Interaction · Planning · Control · Shared Autonomy · Human Augmentation</p></div>
</article>
</div>

<div class="research-bridge"><p>Across all three directions, we integrate <strong>perception, learning, planning, and control</strong> into complete autonomous and interactive robotic systems.</p></div>
</div>
