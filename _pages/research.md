<div class="page-intro">
<p class="section-index">RESEARCH</p>
<h1>Understand. Learn. Collaborate.</h1>
<p>We develop robots that understand humans and the physical world, learn from people, and collaborate with them.</p>
</div>

<div class="focus-list">
<article class="focus-row">
<div class="focus-index">01</div>
<div><p class="focus-kicker">Perceive / Reason / Understand</p><h2>Understand Humans</h2><p>Robots need representations that connect people, places, language, and intent. We study perception and spatial intelligence for understanding humans and dynamic physical environments.</p><p class="topic-line">Robot Perception · Spatial Intelligence · State Estimation · Localization and SLAM · Language Grounding · Scene Understanding</p></div>
</article>
<article class="focus-row">
<div class="focus-index">02</div>
<div><p class="focus-kicker">Observe / Imitate / Generalize</p><h2>Learn from Humans</h2><p>Human demonstrations contain rich knowledge about motion, contact, and task structure. We develop embodied learning methods that transform this knowledge into transferable robot skills.</p><p class="topic-line">Imitation Learning · Learning from Demonstration · Human Motion Understanding · Embodied Learning · Humanoid Locomotion and Manipulation</p></div>
</article>
<article class="focus-row">
<div class="focus-index">03</div>
<div><p class="focus-kicker">Plan / Act / Collaborate</p><h2>Collaborate with Humans</h2><p>We build robots that coordinate decisions and physical actions with people. Our goal is safe, legible, and capable collaboration across shared spaces and tasks.</p><p class="topic-line">Human-Robot Interaction · Shared Autonomy · Language-Guided Robotics · Planning and Control · Physical Human-Robot Collaboration · Human Augmentation</p></div>
</article>
</div>

<section class="research-bridge" aria-labelledby="research-platform-title">
  <p class="section-index">FLAGSHIP PLATFORM</p>
  <h2 id="research-platform-title">Humanoid robots as an integration platform.</h2>
  <p>Humanoid systems bring perception, learning, planning, and control together in environments designed around people. The same methods also support autonomous robots and other embodied systems.</p>
  <p class="research-horizon"><strong>Long-term vision:</strong> human-like embodied intelligence, with android-level systems as a distant horizon.</p>
</section>
